# bluelytics cards: "Editorial Bold", final

Built on b-editorial, the variant the judges scored highest. One huge neutral hero in **Chivo Black** sits on a deep brand-blue field crossed by a tapered **blade** taken from the logo's swept strokes. The blade, its thin sliver (green, red or grey) and the glow (teal dawn at the top for up, wine dusk at the bottom for down) carry the direction. The number stays white, so a bad day never turns into a wall of red. The delta is a solid **slab** with dark ink (about 10:1 up, 6:1 down), an SVG ▲/▼, the sign, the pesos and the percent. Its free end is cut on the diagonal of the move. Unchanged reads "= sin cambios" in grey.

**The blade.** Up cards: it climbs from the left edge through the hero and leaves through the top. Down cards get the mirror: it enters through the top, falls steeply through the right of the hero and levels out into the right edge behind the slab, so a bad day is as dramatic as a good one. Its end runs on 700 px past the edge, so no cut end ever shows. Flat and no-reference cards get a grey blade from the same curve family as the up blade, only shallower. Its edges stay out of the hero's middle third and its thin line stays off the digits altogether, so it can never read as a strikethrough of the price. Depending on the layout it runs along the bottom band (story, og), under the hero (portrait weekly), or across the top corner (landscape and the denser portrait cards).

**Blade routing.** Each layout registers the box of every text it places, plus the logo, the tag and the chart. Then the router searches the routes near the preferred one for each size and direction, trying thinner blades if needed. It keeps the first route where the sliver touches nothing, the body neither cuts nor covers any small text (muted mono on the blade's brand blue would drop to about 2.5:1) and the body edge stays 24 px clear of hatched empty-day cells. Only the hero digits, the superior "$", the opaque slab and the filled no-reference chip may be crossed (by up and down blades). The sliver fades in over the first 42 % of the blade, so its tail never shows as a stub at the left edge. Long kickers, 6-digit prices and year-crossing weeks get a different route instead of a collision. When no clean route exists, the sliver is dropped. Each candidate is screened with a coarse 8-segment ribbon first, so the worst render takes a few ms (budget: 10 ms, asserted by render-extra). Sliver thickness, opacity and glow scale with √|move| (0,06 % is quiet, ≥ 3 % is full).

**Tokens** (`TOKENS` in card.mjs): ink `#040A14`, navy `#06203D`, brand blue `#0069AC`/`#005E9D`, lift `#1C7FD6`, chart line `#7DBEFF`, text `#F4F8FC`, soft `#C3D6EA` (keys), muted `#93A9C2` (notes), ghost `#6F87A2` (days without a close, ≥ 4.5:1), slab ink `#03121F`, up `#2FD67B`, down `#FF5D5D`, flat `#A9B8CA`. Glow: up `#08607E`, down `#6E1430` at 60 % strength (a crimson cast, not the earlier mauve `#3A2148`).

**Type, three tiers.**
- Loud: Chivo 900 hero (≤ 300 px; fitted to width, so 6 digits shrink instead of clipping; superior "$" at 46 % in soft) and slab.
- Labels: Chivo 700 caps with +8 % tracking (kicker, stat keys); values in Chivo 700, fitted per row.
- Quiet: Chivo Mono 500, sentence case, no tracking, for chart notes, axis values, day labels, "vs. cierre anterior", the timestamp and the domain.

Both families are OFL, from Omnibus-Type in Buenos Aires. Total WOFF2 is 40 KB. Minimum text size is 34 px on 1600-wide cards and 30 px elsewhere.

**Kinds**
- `update`: "DÓLAR BLUE · VENTA", hero, slab and "vs. cierre anterior" (one line; beside the slab when it fits, else under it), then the 30-day chart. The chart has its period above it ("30 ruedas · 12 oct – 20 nov", or "1 rueda · 20 nov" for a single close, in which case the plot is left out and the stats close up under the caption), the real max and min on dotted guides with mono values in a right gutter, the last segment and end dot in the move colour, and a minimum span of 4 % of the price. Below it: Compra, Oficial, Brecha, timestamp (ART) and domain.
- `weekly`: a solid white "RESUMEN SEMANAL" tag at the end of the logo row (it tells weekly from update even in a 125 px thumbnail) and "DÓLAR BLUE · <range>", the last close against the previous week, and a day-cell chart: "previo" (last week's close as a ghost point plus a dotted reference level), then lun–vie with "lun 16" labels. Days without a close are hatched and never bridged by a line; only adjacent days are joined. The span is at least 2 %. Value labels are quiet Chivo Mono, with only the last close in bold white. Each label sits on the side of its point away from the 'previo' level. Labels collide when they share a line band and are closer than 0.6 em (plus 8 px padding each); then only the last, previo, the max, the min and the first may keep theirs, in that priority, and one that still clashes with a higher-priority label is dropped. On landscape a shrunken 6-digit hero still keeps the chart's bold top label at least 20 px below the 300 px hero's baseline. The last weekly segment is deliberately not coloured by the move: the move is against last week's close, so colouring Thursday to Friday would misstate it. The end dot carries the colour instead. Below it: Mínimo, Máximo, Oficial, Brecha.
- `og`: "DÓLAR BLUE HOY", hero, slab, lockup and domain. The domain shares the slab's text baseline and hangs under a hairline level with the slab's top, so the bottom row reads as one line. No small print, everything inside the 1200x600 band.

**Layouts.** Landscape has a main column (hero, chart) and a sidebar (a two-row slab with a tall arrow, set cap-to-baseline with the hero, then stat rows), sharing one mono notes row. Portrait and story use one column with 72 px side margins (safe in the 3:4 grid crop). The story hero block sits inside y 270–1248 and the footer sits on the last safe line (baseline y 1570); the story rhythm is tightened so its chart is as tall as the portrait one (about 390 px). Below the footer, the story gets a faint brand-blue floor light (down cards already have their dusk there), and every card has a 4.5 % grain over the glows so the large smooth gradients do not band after Instagram recompresses them. The kicker is anchored to the logo row and the hero hangs from a fixed cap line, so a shrunken 6-digit hero leaves its spare room below itself rather than above. Stat keys and values both shrink to keep 36 px clear of each divider.

**No reference close.** If `prevSell` is missing, `computeCardFacts` returns `direction: 'unknown'` and `hasRef: false`. The card then shows an outlined "sin cierre previo" chip on a solid ink fill (so it reads the same on the field and on the blade), with no arrow, no colour and no "vs." note, and the alt text says nothing about a move. It never claims "sin cambios".

**Grafted from the other variants.**
- From a-terminal: the minimum chart span; a mono technical footer and notes (here Chivo Mono, so it stays in the Chivo family); forced es-AR grouping (`useGrouping: 'always'`).
- From c-chart: the weekly day-cell table with "previo", hatched empty days and dated weekday labels; the last chart segment in the move colour; "1 point" grammar handled.

**Exports.**
- Input handling: `computeCardFacts` coerces numeric strings (`"1675"`, as JSON or D1 decimals may arrive) for blue buy/sell, oficial sell, `prevSell` and `series[].sell`, and throws one clear error when `blue.sell` is not a positive number. A missing or unparsable `updatedAt` becomes `null`: the card drops its stamp and the alt text its time sentence, and neither throws.
- `renderCardHtml` (throws for a kind/size pair that does not exist), `computeCardFacts` (`deltaAbs`, `deltaPct`, `direction`, `hasRef`, `gapPct`, `min`, `max`, `intensity`, …) and `cardAltText` (one Spanish sentence, at most 420 characters, ASCII minus, full month names, "hora de Argentina", "Fuente: bluelytics.com.ar." on og, "Un solo cierre en la semana." for a one-day week).
- Formatters: `formatPesos`, `formatSignedPesos`, `formatPercent` (adds a decimal rather than print "+0,00%"), `formatGap` (always one decimal: "0,0%", never "0,04%" or "−0,0%"), `formatTimestamp`, `formatDateRange` (`prose` option), `plainText`, and `TOKENS`.

**Files.**
- `build-fonts.mjs` (`--download` fetches the pinned Fontsource 5.3.0 files) writes `fonts.mjs`. Licences are in `fonts/OFL-*.txt`.
- `extra-scenarios.mjs` and `render-extra.mjs` cover 57 stress renders and write them to `out-extra/` (with `_contact.jpg` and `_alt.txt`). The cases are 6 digits, ±1 peso, −7 %, 1, 3 and flat series, negative brecha, a missing previous close, one-day, holiday and year-crossing weeks, and "sept". `--main` also checks the harness matrix. The checks cover safe areas, text overlap, a dot touching text, the blade crossing or covering small text, NaN, alt-text length and the 10 ms render budget.
- `tools/metrics.mjs` measures the embedded faces' advance widths in Chrome, to check the width table in card.mjs after a font change.
- `tools/sheets.mjs` rebuilds `out/_contact.jpg`, `_thumbs.jpg` and `_grid.jpg` with inlined images, because the harness sheets come out blank.

**Limits.**
- Widths come from a measured glyph table, not live measurement, so a font change needs `tools/metrics.mjs` to be re-run and the table in card.mjs updated.
- On tall up cards (portrait, story) the blade mostly lives in the upper right, because the logo, kicker and "$" fill the upper left.
- A landscape card with a single close leaves the main column empty under its caption; this is rare (the first post after an outage).
- Mono notes at 34 px are wide; the landscape weekly card is the densest layout.
- The slab assumes moves fit in about 8 characters ("+$ 8.125"). Larger strings shrink the slab text, with a floor of 40 px.
- On flat and no-reference landscape cards (and the denser portrait ones) the only clean blade route is across the top corner, so the blade is small there and carries no visible line.
- The story's bottom 340 px (below the last safe line, mostly under Instagram's reply bar) carries only the floor light, the dusk glow or, on flat cards, the blade; nothing essential may go there.
- Up blades may still thread the hero's counters (steeply, so they read as a sweep, not a strike).
